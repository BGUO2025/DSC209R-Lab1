MLBB Hero Radar Chart

How to Use the Radar Chart:

  • Use the dropdowns or search inputs above to select up to 5 heroes.
  • Hover over a legend item or radar polygon to highlight a hero.
  • Click the "Reset All" button to clear all selections.
  • The stats panel on the right shows each hero's detailed attributes, win rate, and release year.
